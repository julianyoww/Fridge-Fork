# Recipe sourcing and review policy

The 40 recipe records in this release are independently composed, AI-assisted drafts for Fridge & Fork. They draw on general cooking methods rather than copied recipe publications. They have **not been kitchen-tested**. No third-party recipe photography is included. This is not a guarantee of legal clearance in every jurisdiction or a claim of exclusive rights to common dishes.

## Publication records

`recipe-provenance.json` and every recipe's `provenance` object record the authoring method, date, origin, rights note, testing status, and empty reviewer/test-date fields. Do not label a recipe tested until a human cooks it and records results. Recipes use 2-serving base quantities, with explicitly optional ingredients. Estimated times are not guarantees.

## Human review gate

Before promoting any draft to reviewed: cook the base serving size; weigh ingredients; check yield, time, taste, equipment and instructions; verify storage and temperature guidance; document variations and allergens; record tester and test date; retest substantive changes. Batch size adjustments need validation too. AI assistance is not a cooking test.

## New sources and contributors

Prefer independently developed recipes. For contributed content obtain written permission for commercial distribution, editing, translations, offline storage, and the actual images. Retain contributor identity and the permission record privately. For licensed datasets, record the exact license, version, access date, commercial permissions, storage limits, and required attribution. Do not import a collection simply because it is accessible online. Do not copy prose or photos, or assume attribution grants permission. Seek qualified legal review of the sourcing policy before a broad commercial launch.

## Matching promises

Presence alone is not quantity verification. Unknown quantities and incompatible units cannot yield “Ready to cook.” Cooked and uncooked rice remain distinct. Generic minced meat requires a type. Weight/volume conversions use no guessed densities. US cup = 240 ml, tablespoon = 15 ml, teaspoon = 5 ml; weight ounces are not fluid ounces. Optional ingredients and unselected substitutions do not affect readiness. Two-dish checks sum shared required ingredients for both recipes.

## Feedback and privacy

No pantry, recipe history, or feedback is uploaded automatically. GitHub feedback links are voluntary, public, and require a GitHub account to submit. Avoid personal or medical details. Saved recipes and grocery lists stay on the current browser; clearing browser data removes them. No cross-device synchronization is promised.
